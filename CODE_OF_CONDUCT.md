# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want contributing here to be a good experience for everyone, regardless of
experience level, background, gender identity, disability, appearance, ethnicity,
religion, or anything else. Most people contributing to this repository are doing
open source for the first time. That shapes how we behave.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith
- Be patient with questions that seem basic. Everyone was there once, including you
- Give feedback on the code, not the person: "this loop runs one extra time" not
  "you clearly did not think about this"
- Accept feedback the same way. A change request is not an insult
- Credit people for their work

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Publishing someone's private information
- Dismissing or mocking beginners
- Deliberately derailing discussions or sustained disruption
- Plagiarising someone's work or claiming their contribution as your own

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, commit messages,
code reviews, chat groups — and at in-person events run by
GITHUB Community GITAM.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness something, message a maintainer listed in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) directly (for example
[@pushpam2404](https://github.com/pushpam2404) on GitHub), rather than posting
in a public thread. Reports are handled confidentially.

You will be believed, you will not be asked to justify why it bothered you, and
you will not be pushed to interact with the person involved.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they judge appropriate:

1. **A private word** — for a one-off that was probably careless
2. **A warning** — with an explanation of what needs to change
3. **A temporary ban** — from the repos and community spaces
4. **A permanent ban** — for serious or repeated violations
